For how the existing options performed, the raw inner product had the worst performance with the lowest amount of recommendations that matched the query genre at 9.3%. In comparison, the TF-IDF cosine had the most amount matched to the query genre at 22%. In comparison, I had used a blended cosine method where I would use the averages of the TF-IDF and raw cosine similarities to compensate for raw cosine overemphasizing common words and TF-IDF overemphasizing the unique words to allow for all words to be valid in song performance. However, this only partially did better. It had a higher amount of recommendations match query genre than raw cosine and raw inner product at 21.7% compared to raw inner's 9.3% and raw cosine's 19.6%. However, the blended cosine performed worse when compared to the TF-IDF cosine method so the new method doesn't always outperform an existing method.
